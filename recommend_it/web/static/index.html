    About this project

    Recommend-It is a content-based film recommender with a legal "where to watch" lookup. Pick a film you like and it finds others that share its genres, directors, cast and subjects, then shows where each one streams in your country, with free options first.

    How it works

    • Data: 300,000+ films from Wikidata (CC0), rebuilt weekly by a GitHub Actions job through batched, rate-limit-aware SPARQL queries.
    • Model: every film becomes weighted tokens: genres, plus people and subjects as Wikidata IDs, so two actors with the same name never get mixed up. These are vectorised with TF-IDF and cached on disk.
    • Ranking: cosine similarity is computed for just the selected film (one sparse row, about 50 ms over 300k films), then blended with a popularity prior.
    • Search: exact, then word-prefix, then typo-tolerant fuzzy matching. Ties go to the more popular film.
    • Where to watch: TMDB / JustWatch availability per country, plus public-domain films on the Internet Archive. Pirate sites are never linked.
